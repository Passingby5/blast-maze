BlastMaze

Break crates. Grab power-ups. Outsmart the bots.

Battle Mode

Last robot standing wins the round. First to 2 rounds wins the match.

Players on this device

2 players share one keyboard: Player 1 uses W A S D and Space, Player 2 uses the arrow keys and Enter.

Computer robots

How to Play

  • Drop a bomb. It goes off in a plus shape and breaks crates.
  • Bombs can set off other bombs. Stay out of the line!
  • Story: clear every critter, then step on the glowing portal.
  • Phone: drag the left stick to move, tap the big button to drop a bomb.
  • Keyboard: arrows or W A S D to move, Space to drop, P to pause.
